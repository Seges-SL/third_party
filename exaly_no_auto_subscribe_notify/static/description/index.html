No "You have been assigned" Notification

Do not send 'You have been assigned' notification to followers of documents.

Configure all the models for which you do not want to send the nofication when assigning a user to a record (Sale Order, Invoice, ...)
Once done, the notification will not be sent anymore to the salesperson even if the salesperson is added as a follower of the record.
